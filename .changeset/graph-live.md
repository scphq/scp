The relay dashboard's graph streams live: a member's same-origin event socket carries presence, endpoint changes and message metadata (never a body), so a message pulses along its edge as it is sent. A full-window view at `/orgs/<handle>/graph` suits a big screen. The layout settles once and holds still, machines are soft regions joined through the hub, and harnesses show their own marks. The hub gains a `dashboard` hook and `dashboardEvent`.

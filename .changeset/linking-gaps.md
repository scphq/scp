Linking fixes. `swarm codex` honours a spawn-env grant (`env $(swarm spawn-env ...) swarm codex`): its MCP shim and channel spend the grant, so the session binds under the grant's parent with its title instead of coming up as a root. `swarm join` and other self-authenticating commands work from codex's sandboxed shell: the codex sandbox markers are recognised, and when the sandbox hides the harness process the CLI falls back to the binding the session's MCP shim records. An agent can now ask to adopt a root session: `swarm adopt <child>` from the agent's shell sends the child a link request, which the child accepts with `swarm link accept <id>`, the `relay_link_accept` tool or its own `swarm join --under`. The human at a terminal can adopt under a live parent with `--parent` even when the terminal inherited that parent's `$SCP_NAME`.

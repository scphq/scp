Under `SCP_NATIVE=1` the native `swarm` also runs `swarm claude` itself: the foreground launch with the scp channel, `--bg` with its mount record and short-id link, `--resume` of a background session, and `claude attach`, with the same grant attribution, `--under` placement and output as the TypeScript CLI. The router no longer limits `swarm claude` to `ls` and `prune`.

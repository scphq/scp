A send or ask refused as unreachable from a root you act as by name (`--as` or `$SCP_NAME`), as a job a detached script runs becomes, now says why and what to do: a root reaches its own tree and the roots only, and acting by name proves nothing about the agent that started the job, so the job needs a place under that agent, from a `spawn-env` grant minted in the agent's own shell or by linking with `SCP_UNDER`. The refusal itself is unchanged and still reads like an unknown address.

A background session that a harness daemon hosts, and resumes or revives in a process of its own, keeps its name, place and mail: a claim with no grant that lineage places nowhere takes back the released row of the same harness session, a holder whose process ended counts as released, and a session's own hook endpoint is never its lineage parent. `swarm claude --bg --resume` reuses the name, grant and parent request of the session's previous mount, also when the resume forks a new session id. `swarm relink` puts an auto-named root back on the endpoint its grant named and placed, and a takeover by a root moves it under that row's parent, bound by that row's caps as well as its own, and takes the grant that row spent. A shim whose session's hook endpoint is still connected is never placed under that endpoint.

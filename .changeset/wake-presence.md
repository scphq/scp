A wakeable endpoint whose waker lives shows `idle`, never `offline`: the connection that set its wake (the MCP shim, the pi extension, the OpenCode plugin's waiter, the Codex launcher) counts as its waker until it closes or sets `none`, a hook waker for `LIMITS.restartGraceS` more while its harness starts the next one. `list` keeps such endpoints without `--all`, and the broker reports the waker's leaving like other presence changes. The hub and other machines show a live mirrored row with the presence its machine sent, not one its `last_seen` decays to.
